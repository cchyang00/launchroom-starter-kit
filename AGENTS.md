# Launchroom: our whole workflow

**Launchroom** (http://localhost:3000/p/merrivy/) is not a place to log work. **It is the workflow.** Every step of the business, from finding a product to launching ads, runs through it:
- you find your job there;
- you do it the way its step says;
- you save the result where it reads it;
- the step's gate turns green there;
- the owner moves the product on there.

Work that isn't in Launchroom didn't happen: the owner can't see it, the next agent can't build on it, and the workflow can't get better. The owner, October 1: "The only way to improve our workflow is to actually use it."

Every command below is `node .claude/skills/merrivy-os/scripts/os.mjs <command>`, written here as `os.mjs`. The helper keeps its old "merrivy-os" folder name for now; `os.mjs help` lists every command.

**How we work** (owner, November 5): Launchroom stays open next to whichever agent chat we use (Claude Code, Codex or the Gemini agent in Antigravity). The chat is where the owner talks; **this file and the workflow make every agent do the work through Launchroom**, and every tool lives in the agent browser behind **Connect** (*"the connect connects to all the tools ... All those tools, to me, should just be all in the browser. Once you sign in, the agent verifies and turns on that connection to all your tools. Then it knows how to work, and it will open those dedicated tabs to work in."*).

## 1. Start every session

0. **Check Connect first:** `os.mjs connect`. CONNECTED means the agent browser runs, and the list shows which tools are connected. If it is off, ask the owner to press Connect (`#/connect`) or run `os.mjs connect start`. After the owner signs in somewhere, run `os.mjs connect check`: Launchroom visits each site in the agent browser and turns the connection on. Work only in a tool that shows connected, and open your working tab with `os.mjs connect open <site>`.
1. Read **[USER.md](USER.md)** (who the owner is and how they work), then **the newest day in [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md)**.
2. **Get your brief from Launchroom.**
   - The manager (the main chat) runs `os.mjs status`.
   - A team agent runs `os.mjs brief --agent <id>`.
   - It opens with the pipeline and its focus, then Now, your lessons and your open to-dos. Follow those lessons.
3. Run `os.mjs doctor`. Fix what it flags or report it to the manager.
4. Run `os.mjs step`: the step in focus, its rules and its prompts. Then `os.mjs pipeline` (every product's line) and `os.mjs product <L###|P###>` for the product you're on.
5. Open the page the owner sees (Home, Pipeline) in the built-in browser, so you know what they know.

## 3. Every job, the same loop

| When | What you do in Launchroom | Command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Find the job's to-do, or add one with an owner and a next step. Read what the Hubs hold for this work first | `os.mjs todo` · `os.mjs add "Verb the thing" --who agent --next "..." --product L###` · `os.mjs hub copy` (or `creatives`, `winners`, `research`, `store`, `team`) |
| Start | Report the job on the Agents tab and post your plan as one event | `os.mjs report <job-id> --agent <id> --status running --title "..." --event "Plan: 1 … · 2 …" --product L###` (role and summary fill in themselves) |
| During | Post an event when each step starts or ends, and at least every 10 minutes | `os.mjs report <job-id> --event "Step 3/5: what you're doing, what's left"` |
| End | Finish the report with links to what you saved; mark the to-do done; write one diary line; propose a lesson or say "nothing new" | `--status completed --ref <file>` · `os.mjs done <id> --log "..."` · `os.mjs diary "..."` · `os.mjs lesson propose ...` |
| Check | Open the page the owner would look at and confirm it shows the truth | Built-in browser: Pipeline, the product page, Agents, To-do |

**Launchroom has no buttons that tell agents what to do** (owner, November 4: "It's not going to necessarily be a thing where I try to click buttons to tell agents what to do, like Launch Agent, because I have the chat for a reason, and it understands Launchroom."). The owner looks and approves there, and gives agents work in the chat.

**Owner decisions are never made by agents:** Save, Pass or Reject; Build this one; issue picks; approvals. **When the owner says one in chat, the manager records it** with `os.mjs owner-said <decision> <id> --quote "<their exact words>"`, so the owner never has to click it again (owner, October 3: "if you cant run the pipeline by yourself and i have to manual this process all the time. clearly something is wrong"). Never record a decision the owner didn't say. **A failed check is different:** save it as failed with its evidence and the product leaves the line by itself, with no owner click (owner, October 3: "i shouldnt have to manually click through too. if the checks dont pass. you can fail the product"). Unknown or blocked is never a fail.

**When Launchroom is wrong or awkward, that's a bug in Launchroom, not something to explain around.** It applies to a wrong step, a stale to-do, a missing button or a command that fails. Tell the manager; the manager fixes it through the os-builder the same day. That's how the workflow improves.

**Automatic Pipeline Progression & Live Meta/Shopify Data-Integrity Sync** (owner, November 4: "Can you help me understand how we can make it so our agents work in the launch room all the time and actually move the products through the pipeline without me asking and do it automatically? When it does the managing of the tab, it also works from the launch room and can grab the data and put it in here automatically and do it in a smart way. I just want the agents to work properly because if it gives me the wrong data then I see the wrong data"):
- **Agents advance every non-owner step automatically by saving its verified gate checks (`PATCH /api/research`, `PATCH /api/products/:id`, `POST /api/launch-checklists`) before ending a job:**
  1. **Product & Verify (`F1–F6`, `A1–A3`, `P1–P5`):** Completed 100% upfront by `scout` + `buyer-researcher` + `checker`. Any failed check auto-fails the card out of the line; any card the owner clicks **Save** on advances straight to **Ready to build** (`Verify` passed).
  2. **Store & Creatives (`C1–C4` + `B1–B5`):** As soon as `copywriter`, `image-maker`, and `store-builder` finish building the creatives in `#/assets` and the storefront packet in `#/build`, they must immediately verify `claims-rights`, `creative-assets`, `angle-offer`, `draft-page`, `service-promise`, `checkout`, and `tracking` on `/api/products/:id` and tick items `1–7` on `/api/launch-checklists` so the product automatically moves through **Checklist (`7/7`)** to **Launch (`#/launch`)**.
  3. **Checklist (`7/7`) -> Launch (`#/launch`) -> Manage (`#/manage`):** Once all 7 checklist items pass (`7/7`) and the owner launches the campaign in `#/launch`, `experiment-plan` and `authorized-test` are verified (`scripts/sync_pipeline_checks.py`), moving the product straight out of the Build & Launch sidebar and into **Manage (`#/manage`)**.
- **Live Meta & Shopify Auto-Sync + Data-Integrity Guards (`scripts/sync_meta_campaigns.py` & `POST /api/meta-campaigns { action: 'sync' }`):**
  - The Launchroom server (`os/server/hub.mjs`) automatically runs `scripts/sync_meta_campaigns.py` every 15 minutes in the background (and on-demand via the **Sync Live Meta & Shopify Now** button in `#/manage`).
