## Say this, not this

| Where | Say this | Not this | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meta headline | You Have to Get This 2027 Journal — Get Yours Now Today! | Your 2027 Grimoire | The video already shows the product. The title has to hook. |
| Meta headline | This Holiday Dress Is Chef's Kiss — Get Yours Now Today! | Evergreen & Classic Red Tartan · Sizes S–XL | A testimonial with urgency, not specs. |
| Primary text | I literally gasped when I opened mine... | 120 gsm vintage-cream pages, 360° lay-flat brass spiral | Sound like a real buyer raving to a friend. Say how it feels. |
| Link description | Almost Sold Out for Christmas · Get Yours Now Today! | A list of features | Every line keeps the urgency. |
| Image hook | First the bottled water at the store is gone. Then the food shelves empty. Next the power cuts out. When night falls on your street, what will you do? | With everything happening in the government... | The viewer is unaware. A vague line makes them ask "what's happening?" and scroll past. |
| Everyday product | How much of this past year did you already forget, and what do you have right now to keep 2027 from blurring away too? | A planner for staying organized | Turn a plain product into a real human feeling. |
| Problem-aware static (#1–#10) | A 6-stage military-grade filtration process removes glass shards, rust, and bacteria down to 0.01 micron when the grid goes down | A random lifestyle photo with a generic water filter slogan | Listen to the winning viral videos first and turn their spoken mechanism and hidden threat into the static. |
| Solution-aware static (#11–#20) | Military-Grade 6-Stage Electric Pump (1L in 60s) vs. Sucking Muddy Water Through a Plastic Straw on Your Knees | Portable outdoor water filter | Use competitor research and 4–5 star reviews to prove why our mechanism is way better than theirs. |
| High-ticket hardware title & hook | Military-Grade 6-Stage 0.01-Micron Electric Water Purifier · Turn Any Water Into Pure Drinking Water With One Touch | One-Touch Portable Water Filter · Turn any water into pure drinking water at one touch | "One-touch" alone feels weak for a $129.99 system, and "at one touch" is broken grammar. Lead with Military-Grade authority and natural English ("with one touch"). |
| Store title | The Witching Hour 2027 Grimoire Planner | You have to get this 2027 planner | The ad already did that job. The store sounds like a boutique. |

## What makes a good ad

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 20 statics from winning viral videos (10 problem-aware + 10 solution-aware) | Lazy rippers only download videos and hope. We run the top 1–3 viral videos PLUS 20 statics built from those videos' spoken mechanisms (`winning-statics` & `sbc-statics-2.0`). |
| 10 Problem-Aware statics (#1–#10: `F8`, `F2`, `F7`, `F1`) | Make cold scrollers aware of a hidden problem or domino-chain crisis they never knew existed (e.g., grid collapse -> contaminated water -> glass shards & minerals). |
| 10 Solution-Aware statics (#11–#20: `F4`, `F3`, `F5`, `F6`/`F9`) | Built from competitor research and 4–5 star Amazon reviews to prove why our product's mechanism is way better than competitors'. |
| `sbc-statics-2.0` copy pack (Concept Ledger + 5 evergreen captions + 5 headlines) | Every 20-static batch includes a Concept Ledger (`feature -> what it does -> practical benefit -> why it matters in their day`), 5 distinct 250–400w captions, and 5 short 4–9w headlines. |
| A 3–7 word headline | It has to grab in under a second. Our first ads ran 14–20 words. |
| Urgency, then the 3 best features | They should be sold on the product before they click. |
| First person: "I bought this..." | A store's voice reads as an ad. A person's voice doesn't. |
| Built from real reviews | Amazon and TikTok Shop buyers already said who it's for and what they love. Never guess. |
| Harvest the viral video's words | The top videos already reveal the product in its best light. Combine their words with our offer. |
| The real product, from one reference ad | Copy one layout that works, with our exact product in it. |
| No coupon boxes on the image | It sounds desperate. Let the story carry the pressure. |
| No small-print diagrams | Squinting looks like interest to Meta, so it spends money on an ad nobody clicks. |

## Try next

| Idea | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Build 20 video-derived statics (10 problem-aware + 10 solution-aware) on the next launch | Listen to the winning viral videos' spoken mechanism and pair 10 unaware problem ads with 10 competitor-crushing solution ads so we beat video-only rippers. |
| Rebuild our winners with a 3–7 word headline and a 3-features strip | DR25, C11 and C10 had the story but no quick features on the ad. |
| Post from avatar pages (Olivia Scott, Michael L) in first person | Our two best Planner statics were already written in first person. |
| Test the domino chain and the specific fact against the countdown | DR27, DR29 and DR28 against DR24 on Solar. The campaign stopped before this got an answer. |
| Test the feeling triggers against the viral videos | DR26 memory, DR27 self-expression and DR28 racing mind against V01 and V02 on the Planner. Also unanswered. |
| Keep each ad set to 3–5 ads, with new batches in their own set | Early momentum starves the rest at $0.00. |
| Keep yesterday's winners running and build beside them | Turning off a selling video made Meta re-learn and pushed CPM from $54.64 to $68.38. |
