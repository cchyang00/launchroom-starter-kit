## Counter-evidence (against passing) and limits

- The classroom wrap lane is thin, not proven empty: Meta's text search cannot hear speech in a video, and the all-words queries were sorted by impressions, so I read the biggest 44 to 181 ads each (a third or less of the large totals). A video ad about a cold classroom would only show if its caption said so.
- Teacher-gift cardigans already sell to teachers on Meta (3 sellers). They do not claim warmth or a cold room, but a buyer shopping "cozy teacher layer" meets them first.
- The hot-room avatar (B) found nothing: `hot classroom`, `classroom fan` and `teacher cooling` returned no product for teachers.
- Meta's own "~N" counts are estimates; 0 means no active ad matched, not that none ran before.
- Wearable-blanket sellers who target "cold at the office" (not teachers) run ads on the same audience: see the L130 Prove it file, [Room on Meta](../../../research/2026-10-03-verified-issues/prove/L130.md). They do not say "classroom".
