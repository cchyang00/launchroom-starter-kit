# Issue: "I am always freezing in my classroom" / "my classroom sits at 80 degrees" (schoolteachers in a room they can't control)

*One file per issue: `resources/issues/teachers/ISSUE.md`. Method and gate: [workflow/CHECKS.md](../../../workflow/CHECKS.md), Step 1. Researched 2026-10-03 by buyer-researcher (job issues-gemini-hunt-2026-10-03, group: schoolteachers on their feet all day). Status: Candidate (I1 to I5 ticked; Bluesky carries 28 of 32 quotes, and a plug-free product cannot fix a 95-degree room). Every quote has a link and a date; unknown stays unknown.*

**Why this issue and not the others (read first):** foot, shoe, insole, sock and mat talk is the crowded mainstream corner, so I skipped it. Of the non-foot rows, two had real posts: the bathroom and water bind (about 20 first-person posts on Bluesky, X1 to X5 below) and the room temperature (28 Bluesky posts, 3 press quotes and 1 UK line). I chose temperature because a small physical product can answer it (a plug-free way to stay warm or cool in a room the teacher cannot change), while no object answers "no time to pee" (a schedule problem). The cart/bag row had one thin post, voice strain a few posts about losing the voice with no object attached, and the Sunday-night wind-down about a dozen posts that are about dread, not a product.

**Honest limits:**
- **Read:** Bluesky public search (api.bsky.app, no login, posts since 2024-10-03, about 630 posts saved; links use the account ID, never a handle), 3 US news pages with teacher quotes, YouTube comments on about 70 videos, City-Data Teaching forum, Lemmy, Hacker News, Mumsnet (UK).
- **Blocked or empty:** ProTeacher (a "verify you're not a robot" JavaScript page, not bypassed). TES community now redirects to tes.com/community and returns 403. Reddit, Quora, Facebook not tried. DuckDuckGo showed a challenge page, left alone. public.api.bsky.app returned 403 (I used api.bsky.app, which answered without a login). City-Data Teaching, Lemmy teacher communities (18 to 31 comments in total) and Hacker News had nothing on topic. The YouTube comments were students and viewers, no teacher on topic. Mumsnet is open but UK: its teacher clothing threads talk about cardigans for changing room temperatures, mostly before Oct 2024; one Feb 2026 thread gave a single thin line (#U1).
- **Weak spots:** 28 of 32 quotes are one platform (Bluesky), which skews online and progressive, and posts carry no country (most use Fahrenheit; I dropped two that looked non-US). The 3 press quotes are reporter-mediated and #U1 is UK. No open US teacher forum was readable, so "where they talk" is partly a guess. Typos and rough language are the writers'.

## 1. The issue in one line

A teacher spends the whole day in a room whose temperature is not hers to set: 53 degrees at 7:30, 90 after the fix, or 80 with 32 kids and a box fan. "I am always freezing in my classroom ... also I'm not allowed to have a space heater." (#B3, Bluesky, 2025-01-22)

## 2. What it feels like

- **Powerless:** "I was told that the classroom thermostats were dummies, the heat/ac system was controlled downtown" (#B20)
- **Worn down and needing proof:** "I feel validated now" after buying a thermometer (#B3)
- **Resigned humor:** "Please send cold beverages." (#B19)
- **Not herself in it:** "I own a million cute dresses. My classroom is too cold to wear any of them." (#B15)
- **Collective misery:** "We’d all be cold & cranky or sweaty and sleepy." (#B28) · "Nobody feels good, ... Nobody’s learning." (#P2)

### The moment and its cost (I2)

**The moment that keeps coming back:** unlocking the room before the first bell to a number on the thermometer: "the icebox at 7:30" (#B9), "53 degrees" (#B1), or "95 degrees for a week" (#B17), then teaching in it all day. It repeats every cold snap and every heat wave, and the building decides.
**What it costs, in their words:** focus ("Truly difficult to concentrate at times", #B13), lost lessons ("I had to cancel classes", #B17), comfort and looking put together (#B15), and out-of-pocket money for fans and heaters (#B11, #B21). Family is not found.
