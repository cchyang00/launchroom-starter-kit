## 1. The issue in one line

Hygienists describe working a full day craned over a chair, back to back, and going home in pain with no time to decompress: "And by the end of the day you’re in agony." ([Mumsnet, 12/01/2025](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/am_i_being_unreasonable/5250302-dentist-annual-salary), #2).

## 2. What it feels like

- **Pain that accumulates:** "My back, neck, wrists, fingers and shoulders are all totally fucked quite honestly." (#1)
- **Doubt about lasting:** "I’m not sure I’ll make it to retirement" (#1)
- **Regret:** "But if I could go back in time I’d never choose this again." (#5)
- **No room to recover during the day:** "I can have the worst day ever but I need to be smiling." (#4)
- **Being let down by training:** "In theory, you get taught how to treat patients to avoid these issues. Correct posture etc. But in reality it doesn’t work." (#14)
- **Pride in the hands, fear of losing them:** "That's because our hands are our livelihood." (#11)
