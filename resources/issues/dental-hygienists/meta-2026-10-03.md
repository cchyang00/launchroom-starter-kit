## Counter-evidence and limits

- **The lane is open because the audience is small, not only because nobody found it.** A hygienist group is a small slice of Meta; sellers who reach it on Meta tend to be professional-equipment makers (loupes at hundreds of dollars), not stretch or massage products.
- The one 30+ day signal (SurgiTel) is a high-priced device. A $20 to $30 stretch strap, massage ball or finger band is a different buy and has no competitor here, but the buyer evidence in ISSUE.md is also thin: no hygienist says "I want X" and the TikTok numbers are for generic massage balls and grip kits.
- Hygienists also read dentist-targeted ads, and some sellers will not use the word "hygienist". The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 147 to 187 ads of up to ~5,800.
- Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: PASS.** 1 advertiser with ads 30+ days old speaks to a dental clinician's neck and posture; none speaks to the end-of-day body ache with a cheap physical product. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
