## Counter-evidence and limits

- **A shared lane:** shift-nurses, line-cooks and warehouse-pickers all hit the same "feet after a 10 to 12 hour shift on a hard floor" moment, and the same 10 to 15 shoe, sock and insole sellers serve all three. This is one saturated lane, not three.
- The sellers' creatives lean on foot-pain claims (plantar fasciitis, "pain gone in 2 days") that our rules do not allow, so the angle we could use is narrower than what they run; the lane is still bought.
- The open spots are thin: heel grips as a first-month fit fix has no worker-aimed ad, but the mass-copied heel-liner creative shows dropshippers are already flooding the product (15+ domains in a month). Gloves and a clear tote have little buyer evidence in ISSUE.md.
- The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 120 to 211 ads of up to ~8,400; a small seller outside the top by impressions would not show. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL.** At least 12 sellers with ads 30+ days old speak to long-shift foot pain on hard floors; 2 name warehouses. If this issue is tested, use the first-weeks fit framing or the glove lane and re-run I7 on that wording first. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
