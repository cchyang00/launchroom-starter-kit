## Counter-evidence and limits

- **Whole-body warmth is taken.** Heated chairs, suits and base layers have run for 6 to 14 months. A hunter who feels the cold sees them first, and the issue's pitch ("cold feet") is a narrower part of the same fight.
- **The foot-specific lane is open but small.** ISSUE.md notes the ready-made versions are "one small brand" and that hunters cut their own foam. Zero boot-cover sellers on Meta may mean the product sells in hunting shops and on Amazon rather than through Meta ads.
- **A new seller has appeared:** endureheat.com (2 days old) speaks to the feet in the sit and will reach 30 days on Nov 1.
- The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 154 of up to ~16,000 reported. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads. Ice-angler buckets were barely found (ISSUE.md could not prove that avatar either).

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL.** 10 or more sellers with ads 30+ days old speak to a cold long sit, and 3 sock brands speak to cold feet. The foot-platform pad, boot cover and foot muff lanes have no direct competitor on Meta. If this issue is tested, use one of those and re-run I7 on that wording first. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
