## Counter-evidence (against passing) and limits

- **A wave is arriving.** Three sellers launched sweaty-palm ads between Sep 4 and Oct 1, all on the same hook (sweaty palms ruin the aim), and ISSUE.md says the Noctua fan mouse is a hot topic right now. By Oct 4 the first is 30 days old. By the time a product built for this issue launches (4 to 6 weeks), the count of 30+ day ads could be 3.
- **They are dropshipper-style copies.** soyummy.com and reviongrip.com both read as test pages ("Low impression count"; early-bird discounts), so most will not survive; the ones that do will show as 30+ day ads in late October.
- **Meta's text search cannot hear speech in videos**, and finger-sleeve sellers for mobile gamers may not use the words searched. The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 129 of ~760 and 95 of ~580.
- Cold-hand avatar (C): `gaming gloves` found nothing for PC or console players. Unheated fingerless gloves for gamers have no Meta advertiser.
- The mobile-gaming finger-sleeve market (the proven TikTok Shop product in ISSUE.md) has no Meta consumer ads in these searches. That could mean open space, or that those sellers sell on TikTok Shop only.
