## Counter-evidence and limits

- **Open lane inside a taken lane:** no advertiser sells a wake-up light to the "can't get out of bed once it's dark and cold" voice in ISSUE.md's words; they all lead with a gentle vs harsh alarm. That is an angle gap, not a product gap, and the full P1 check on L099 or L098 is where it is judged.
- **FitSleeps runs 94 ads and a creator network**; the vibrating-band lane is being paid for hard. Its ADHD framing is a health-condition claim we may not use.
- **The sunrise-alarm count is partly feature mentions.** Counting only ads that talk about waking up (Dreamegg, Rise Centered, Lumie) gives exactly 3, the failing threshold, so the margin is zero either way.
- The all-words partners were not run for the product phrases (see the searches note). `tired all the time` was read to 144 of ~7,200 ads, so its supplement count of 7 is a floor.
- Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL.** The supplement lane owns the broad words, and the two physical lanes on current cards have 3 advertisers each with ads 30+ days old. Cards L099 (sunrise wake-up light) and L098 (vibrating wrist alarm) should expect P1 to land at "crowded product" at best, and the angle will have to be the open part. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
