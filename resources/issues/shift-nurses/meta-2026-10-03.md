## Counter-evidence and limits

- **This is the crowded corner of the group, as ISSUE.md warned.** The five 30+ day advertisers have run for four to twelve months, which usually means the ads paid back. The lead-moment angle ("12-hour shift feet") is a bought one.
- The open lanes are narrow and rest on thin evidence. The lunch tote has 3 quotes in ISSUE.md; the night-shift sleeper has 1 quote there. A product built on either has the full P1 check to pass.
- Day-sleep for night-shift workers is **not** empty: Guard Blinds (45 d) and a sleep-mask seller (5 d) already speak to nurses and shift workers. Together with the layover day-sleep in the flight-crew file, the day-sleeper moment is one lane across three groups, not three separate lanes.
- The all-words queries are sorted by impressions and were read to between 83 and 155 ads of up to 35,000 reported, so a small seller outside the top by impressions would not show.
- Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL (feet and legs after a 12-hour shift).** 5 advertisers with ads 30+ days old speak to it. A test on this issue should use an open side moment (the locker lunch tote at 0, the night-shift sleeper at 1 of 3), not the shoe, sock or slide lane, and re-run I7 on that specific moment first. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
