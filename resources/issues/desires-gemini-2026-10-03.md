## Gemini's product ideas we do not use
LED face masks, "medical-grade" gua sha, clavicle braces, kinesiology tape, BackJoy-style wedges, breathwork devices (Moonbird), supplements and sleep elixirs, GLP-1 companion foods, coffee gear, haptic posture wearables. These break the rules (devices that imply treatment, anything eaten, batteries or app electronics) or are brand products.

## Gemini ideas worth a later look (unverified leads)
"Analog dopamine menu" boards, undated one-to-three-task daily planners, tactile Pomodoro dials, heavy-bottom desk pads, weighted lap pads. Gemini's own groups: Gen Z and millennial renters, remote and hybrid workers, adult-ADHD communities, perimenopausal and menopausal women, "longevity" Gen X. Treat the ADHD and menopause groups as research context only: never name a condition in copy.
