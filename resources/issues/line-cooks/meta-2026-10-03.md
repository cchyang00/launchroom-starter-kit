## Counter-evidence and limits

- **The shoe lane is taken, as ISSUE.md warned.** MISE has run for 4 months, and Snibbs and Stand are wide "work shoe" sellers with thousands of reviews. A cook who sees an ad for kitchen shoes sees one of these.
- **The count is exactly 3, so the verdict is sensitive.** If the manager treats Hike Footwear's chef persona as a shoe ad rather than a cook ad, the strict count is 2. If Snibbs or Stand count, it is 4 to 5.
- **The accessory lane is open on Meta but thin in buyer evidence.** ISSUE.md has one cook quote for the heel grip and none for the shoe bag, and it notes TikTok shows nothing aimed at cooks. An empty lane here is untested.
- The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 26 to 182 ads of up to ~31,000 reported; a small seller outside the top by impressions would not show. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL (narrow, exactly 3).** Three kitchen-aimed shoe advertisers have ads 30+ days old. If this issue is tested, use the accessory lane (second-pair bag, washable insole, clog heel grip) and re-run I7 on that wording first. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
