# Issue: "tilt my head uncomfortably back" (adult climbers who wear glasses, and belayers whose neck pays for it)

*`resources/issues/climbers-glasses/ISSUE.md`. Method and gate: [workflow/CHECKS.md](../../../workflow/CHECKS.md), Step 1. Researched 2026-10-03 by buyer-researcher (job issues-niche-b-climbers, set B, hobbies). Every quote has a link and a date; unknown stays unknown. Owner direction (Oct 3): non-saturated audiences and pain points that are intriguing and not something everyone has.*

**Read this first (honest verdict).**
- **The belayer-neck / belay-glasses hypothesis is real in the forums but the product is already well served.** Belayers do describe next-day neck cramping (quote 11) and glasses-wearing climbers do struggle with varifocals (1, 4, 21). But Amazon already sells belay glasses at every price: one listing has 2.9K ratings at 4.7 stars, and a competitor title says "Anti Neck Strain" (section 6). This is **not** an untouched audience.
- **The only gap the quotes point at** is narrow: a durable, metal-frame, **clip-on** pair for people who already wear glasses. One forum thread complains the single known clip-on costs about $60, broke in 18 months and "is just a subscription service" (15, 16). Everything else (cases, straps, plain belay glasses) is saturated.
- **Search that came up thin:** UK Climbing had only about 3 belayer-neck mentions in the window (quote 7 is the clearest), and most belay-glasses threads there are for-sale, lost or wanted posts. Hill-walker and wild-camper themes (cold hands, thin mats, condensation, pack straps, midges, wide feet) were read on UKC and are brand-heavy and scattered; none had 10 quotes on one pain tied to a simple unbranded product. They were dropped.

## 1. The issue in one line

For people who climb in glasses or belay for long stretches, the head tilts back to see the leader or the next hold, the neck pays for it, and the fixes (belay glasses, clip-ons, straps, cases) break, slip or cost more than they feel they should: "Having varifocals means I'm trying to use the distance part of the lens which makes seeing difficult unless I tilt my head uncomfortably back." (UK Climbing, 18 Apr 2026)

## 2. What it feels like

- **Neck paying the bill:** "My neck is extremely sensitive to extended periods of bending, to the point where belaying 2-3 pitches without glasses leads to debilitating cramping and soreness the next day." (#11, Mountain Project, 17 May 2025)
- **A sore neck that is simply part of it:** "I also carry beloggles if I'm going to be watching my climber - shitty neck sucks." (#12)
- **Fed up with the price loop:** "$60 every 1.5 years is just a subscription service." (#16)
- **Fed up with kit that snaps:** "I just snapped the leg on my third pair of Metolious belay glasses." (#20)
- **Blind at the worst moment:** "Try climbing with any glasses when one of the lenses drops out. Talk about difficulty focusing. Happened to me halfway up an unprotected HVS/E1!" (#6)
- **Squinting through the wrong part of the lens:** "Traditional varifocal glasses are a nightmare for down-climbing as you're trying to look at your feet through the near-vision part of the lens" (#4)

### The moment and its cost (I2, checked 2026-10-03)

**The moment that keeps coming back:** belaying a leader up steep ground with the head tipped back, or looking up to a hold through the wrong part of a varifocal lens; it repeats on every route.
**What it costs, in their words:** the next day, "My neck is extremely sensitive to extended periods of bending, to the point where belaying 2-3 pitches without glasses leads to debilitating cramping and soreness the next day." (#11, Mountain Project, 17 May 2025); the climb, "Since glasses became a norm, I've had to bail on one lead due to fog, sweat, and smears." (#25, Mountain Project, 21 Nov 2025) and "I can definitely see, but I've found myself missing small footholds more often." (#26, Mountain Project, 3 Oct 2024); and money, "$60 every 1.5 years is just a subscription service." (#16, Mountain Project, 14 Sep 2026). Work and family are not seen in this pass.

**Evidence against (read before choosing this issue):**
- Belay glasses are a mature category on Amazon, from $9.95 to $57, with a 2.9K-rating leader. The neck-strain story is already the headline claim of competitor titles.
