# The desire map: what people want to become, and what we can sell toward it

*Oct 3, 2026 · buyer-researcher · job desire-map-2026-10-03. A map for choosing issues and products, not proof for any one product. Figures were opened at their sources; the Gemini runs are leads only ([desires-gemini-2026-10-03.md](desires-gemini-2026-10-03.md)). Amazon = "bought in past month" badges seen Oct 3. Trends = Google Trends US, last 13 weeks vs the same weeks of 2025. TikTok Shop numbers were not checked: its terms are search ideas.*

**Why a map:** 82% of US consumers call wellness a top or important priority, and sleep is the #2 priority with the most unmet needs (McKinsey, Nov 2024, 5,000+ consumers in China, UK, US). What sells is small, physical and aimed at one moment. Copy never says "you have…" and never promises relief.

## 1. Rested: "I want to wake up and actually feel ready."
- **Steady; masks and earplugs crowded.** Sleep trends for 2026 include "Sleep by Design": light, noise, temperature (Global Wellness Institute, Mar 30 2026). Good sleepers flourish 88% vs 47% of poor sleepers (NSF, Mar 6 2025). Masks run 10K-40K+ bought per listing. Trends: knee pillow +37%, cooling pillow +38%.
- **Bites:** a partner awake at 11 pm ("listening to my H snoring the actual house down", Mumsnet, 8 Jan 2025) · a flat-dweller woken at 5-6 am ("I don't want to be woken up at 5 - 6 am every morning if I have been at work all week", Mumsnet, 19 Apr 2025) · crew and drivers day-sleeping.
- **Issues:** [sleep](sleep/ISSUE.md), [flight-crew](flight-crew/ISSUE.md), [truck-drivers](truck-drivers/ISSUE.md).
- **Search (TikTok Shop, Amazon):** portable blackout curtain suction cup (1-2K+, $23-43) · knee pillow for side sleepers (Contour 10K+; card L101) · zero-pressure blackout mask (L104, crowded).
- **Ad image:** a window still dark at 7:02 am, she wakes before the alarm: "Ready before the day is."

## 2. Unwound: "I want my head to go quiet when the day is over."
- **Rising theme; sunset lamps crowded (10K+).** "The Rise of Neurowellness" and an "Over-Optimization Backlash" lead the Global Wellness Summit's 2026 list (Jan 27 2026). Pinterest (Mar 17 2026): "reset aesthetic" +200%, "comfy reading chair small spaces" +455%. Trends: wearable blanket +64%.
- **Bites:** caregivers once the house is down (63M US family caregivers, +45% in a decade; AARP, Jul 24 2025) · remote workers who close the laptop where they live (35% of workers did some work at home, BLS, Jun 25 2026) · small-flat renters.
- **Issues:** [sleep](sleep/ISSUE.md), [low-energy](low-energy/ISSUE.md). "Mind won't switch off" (#5) has no file or quotes yet.
- **Search:** reading pillow with arms (1-4K, $29-51) · foldable bed lap desk (7K+, $30) · acupressure mat (L102, no relief claims).
- **Ad image:** lamp low, phone out of reach, back on a reading pillow: "The first hour that's hers."

## 3. In control: "I want to leave the house with everything, not scrambling."
- **Rising theme; fridge calendars crowded.** Pinterest (Mar 17 2026): "Sunday reset list" +65%, "laundry room organization small space" +390%, "cleaning is the new self-care". Trends: entryway organizer +30%, dry-erase calendar -16%.
- **Bites:** the daily misplacer ("Every single day I lose something or forget something. I do try hard.", Mumsnet, 19 Mar 2026) · the parent who carries the family's list ("I often keep everything I have to do/everything everyone has on in my head", Mumsnet, May 2026).
- **Issues:** [brain-fog](brain-fog/ISSUE.md) (parked for copy risk: situations only).
- **Search:** entryway key-and-mail wall station (1K+, $19-36) · undated adult wall planner · over-door bag hook rack.
- **Ad image:** hands full of coffee and a bag, keys already on the hook: "Out the door in one go."

## 4. Focused and energised: "I want to sit down and actually get it done."
- **Steady; desk gear crowded.** 35% of employed Americans did some work at home on days they worked in 2025 (BLS, Jun 25 2026). Crowded: desk lamps 9K+, footrests 6K+, sunrise clocks (Hatch $170, 9K+; $34-40 clones). Visual timers are sold "for kids" (Trends -15%). Under-desk pedal +86% on a tiny base.
- **Bites:** the remote worker ("When I work from home I can find it really hard to sit and focus on work", Mumsnet, 21 Jan 2025) · the drained office worker ("By the time I am home, I am very drained mentally and don't feel like doing anything", Blind, 17 Sep 2026).
- **Issues:** [low-energy](low-energy/ISSUE.md), [brain-fog](brain-fog/ISSUE.md).
- **Search:** adult visual timer (gap: listings say "kids") · under-desk pedal, no display · sunrise light (L099, crowded).
- **Ad image:** 9:00, first task open, a red disc of time shrinking beside the keyboard.

## 5. Present: "I want my mornings and nights back from my phone."
- **Real problem, thin product signal.** 50% use a screen in bed daily; 38% say bedtime news-scrolling worsens their sleep (46% of 18-24) (AASM, Feb 23 2026; n=2,007, fielded Jun 2025). Trends: analog alarm clock +32% on a tiny base, phone lock box flat. Lock boxes: about 10 listings.
- **Bites:** a young adult scrolling in bed · a parent scrolling after the kids sleep. No forum quotes yet.
- **Issues:** [sleep](sleep/ISSUE.md); "phone" (#4) is not researched.
- **Search:** solid-wood phone docking station and nightstand (L116) · timed phone lock box ($30-40) · analog alarm clock ($10-15, bundle only).
- **Ad image:** phone docked across the room, a paper book in bed: "Morning back in her hands."

## 6. Keep doing what I love: "I want to finish the whole project without my body calling time."
- **Steady; cheap add-ons crowded, bigger supports a gap.** Hooks, thimbles and cutters are $3-23 with thousands bought. Sewing-machine tilt platforms: unbranded $31.99 (no sales badge) vs Martelli $150-162 (rechecked Oct 3).
- **Bites:** long-hours quilters and knitters ("Facing the possibility of giving up knitting was a horrible thing for me.", KnittingHelp, 6 Dec 2025) · adult violin and viola players · long-haul drivers · treestand hunters.
- **Issues:** [crafters](crafters/ISSUE.md), [musicians](musicians/ISSUE.md), [truck-drivers](truck-drivers/ISSUE.md), [cold-sitters](cold-sitters/ISSUE.md); [dental-hygienists](dental-hygienists/ISSUE.md) is below its gate.
- **Search:** sewing machine tilt platform · craft lap and arm support pillow · violin chin-rest cover, pad and cork-shim bundle.
- **Ad image:** hour three of a quilt, machine tilted toward her, shoulders low: "Finishing what she started."

## 7. Independent: "I want to keep managing my own home, on my own two feet."
- **Large, steady; reachers crowded.** 75% of adults 50+ want to stay in their own home (AARP 2024 survey, fielded Jun-Jul 2024). Reachers 10K+ across many listings (Trends -10%).
- **Bites:** the new retiree rebuilding a routine · an adult child shopping for a parent. No mobility quotes yet (L100, L101 buyers pending).
- **Issues:** [low-energy](low-energy/ISSUE.md) (retiree and carer avatars). No independence issue yet.
- **Search:** folding adjustable cane (L100; FDA lists canes as Class I devices, so keep to "everyday walking") · knee pillow (L101) · easy-grip jar and kitchen helpers.
- **Ad image:** a woman in her seventies on her usual dog loop, cane folded in her tote: "Her morning, her route."

## 8. Confident in how I look: "I want to catch my reflection and like it, without a 40-minute routine."
- **Steady; crowded and cheap, the weakest.** "Skin Longevity" is on the Global Wellness Summit 2026 list (Jan 27 2026). Heatless curl sets 10K+ (Trends +12%), silk pillowcases 6K+ (-4%), scalp massagers $6 (below our price floor).
- **Bites:** busy parents with ten minutes. No quotes, no issue file: research first.
- **Search:** satin pillowcase and bonnet bundle (only a bundle clears $30) · heatless curl set (crowded).
- **Ad image:** a morning mirror, hair set overnight: "Ready in ten."
