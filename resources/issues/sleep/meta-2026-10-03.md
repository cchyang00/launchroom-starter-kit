## Counter-evidence and limits

- **The owner's call is confirmed by the library itself:** the snoring-partner lane that the L097 sleep headband card sits in has had its biggest seller (SnoozeBand) running ads since May 2024, and four other earbud or headband sellers since February. L097 and similar cards should expect P1 to land at "saturated" or "crowded product".
- **What is not here:** the new-parent avatar (A4) and the foggy retired woman (A8) were not searched separately; baby-sleep coaching is a different (children's) product family and the second is covered by the [brain-fog file](../brain-fog/meta-2026-10-03.md).
- The all-words partner was not run for the phrases above, since `cant sleep` already returns 21,000 exact-phrase ads and the other phrases are specific. The queries were sorted by impressions and read to 13 to 174 ads; a small seller outside the top would not show. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.
