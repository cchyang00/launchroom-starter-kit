**I7 rule:** passes when fewer than 3 advertisers already run ads 30+ days old that speak to this group's moment.

**How I counted "speaks to the moment":** the moment in ISSUE.md is the carer's own night and body (a recliner, a hospital chair, aches, no sleep). An ad counts when it sells or speaks to that. Ads that use a bedside carer only as the narrator of a story for a different product, and care services that speak to "burnout" in general, are listed separately so the manager can see both.

## Counter-evidence and limits

- **The audience is bought, for a different promise.** Carers are reached on Meta every week, with "take a break" offers from care providers. A comfort product competes for the same attention and trust, and carers' purchases mostly go to the other person (ISSUE.md says the same).
- **An empty lane here may be an empty need.** ISSUE.md calls product fit "the weak part of this issue": TikTok sells chair cushions and sleep masks in the tens of thousands, but the versions named for carers sell almost nothing. Zero advertisers on Meta is consistent with few buyers who search for it.
- The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to 26 to 155 ads of up to ~3,300; a small seller would not show. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.
- Persona ads show that "a spouse sleeping in a hospital recliner" is a story that works for advertorials; it is not evidence that anyone sells a chair kit.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: PASS (specific moment).** No product advertiser with ads 30+ days old speaks to the carer's own chair night or body. Flag for the manager: care services own the "caregiver burnout" words, so any test should lead with the chair night and the carer's body, not with "burnout". The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
