# Issue: "I can't get any sleep myself and I'm exhausted and full of aches and pains" (family caregivers who sleep, or don't, next to a parent or partner)

*`resources/issues/bedside-caregivers/ISSUE.md`. Method and gate: [workflow/CHECKS.md](../../../workflow/CHECKS.md), Step 1. Researched 2026-10-03 by buyer-researcher (job issues-niche-b-2026-10-03, set B: life stages). Every quote has a link and a date; unknown stays unknown.*

**Group:** adult children and spouses who look after a parent or partner at home (or stay with them in hospital) and whose own nights, back and time pay for it. The forum threads are mostly about dementia care; this file keeps only what the carer says about **their own sleep, body and chair**, never the condition.

**Honest limits (read first):**
- **Product fit is the weak part of this issue.** Most of what carers discuss buying is for the person they care for (hospital bed, hoist, bed pads, bed alarms, baby monitors): medical, electronic or both, so all out for us. Products for the carer's own comfort appear only in passing (earplugs, white noise, a comfy chair). Read section 6 as "worth a fast Find check", not "proven".
- **Read:** AgingCare (about 440 threads from the sleep, burnout, family-caregiver and caregiver-support boards, ids 490000+, Oct 2024 to Oct 2026), Mumsnet Elderly Parents board (about 560 threads), Gransnet Care and Carers board (98 threads), Alzheimer's Society forum (about 580 threads; its post dates looked unreliable on some threads, so only 2 quotes are used). Each quote below was matched word for word against the saved page and its post date read from the page.
- **Not read:** Reddit (r/AgingParents and similar), Quora, MetaFilter, allnurses (blocked), Carers UK forum (nearly dormant: its latest-posts feed held only 50 posts since April 2023), Amazon and TikTok (503 and JavaScript shell to scripts, so the "what exists" check is **not done**).
- Writers sometimes name the other person's condition or their own. That is their wording; ad copy must never use it (section 7).
- Quotes keep the writers' typos. No usernames. Quotes are for learning their words, never for fake reviews.

## 1. The issue in one line

The person who sits up, sleeps in the spare room, the chair or the floor next to someone they look after, and wakes with a sore back and no night's sleep to show for it. In their words: "I can’t get any sleep myself and I’m exhausted and full of aches and pains." (AgingCare, 19 May 2026)

## 2. What it feels like

- **Wired for the next wake-up:** "I sometimes startle awake at his usual times, even when he hasn’t gotten up—just out of habit (which makes it worse because then I might just be getting back to sleep when he does get up)." (#5)
- **Too wound up to sleep even with help:** "I dont get sleep despite an overnight caretaker cuz my mond wont shut off due to the stress.." (#6)
- **Running on almost nothing:** "I got about 2 hours of sleep per day." (#8)
- **Worn out and out of time:** "I have been caring for my husband now for 16 months and am absolutely worn out. I have no time to do what I want or need to" (#14)
- **Cooped up:** "Feeling like a caged tiger at times, while sitting in my armchair" (#16)

### The moment and its cost (I2, checked 2026-10-03)

**The moment that keeps coming back:** 3 a.m. and the person is up again, or a night on a hospital chair, the floor or the sofa. The carer lies awake, or startles awake at the old hours, night after night.
**What it costs, in their words:** sleep, "I got about 2 hours of sleep per day." (#8, AgingCare, 16 Nov 2025) and "I sometimes startle awake at his usual times, even when he hasn’t gotten up" (#5, AgingCare, 23 Apr 2026); sleep even with help, "I dont get sleep despite an overnight caretaker cuz my mond wont shut off due to the stress.." (#6, AgingCare, 23 Nov 2025); the body, "I'd spent several hours in an uncomfortable ER chair with elder." (#4, AgingCare, 23 Jul 2026); and their own time, "I have been caring for my husband now for 16 months and am absolutely worn out. I have no time to do what I want or need to" (#14, Gransnet, 24 Feb 2026). Money is not found in these quotes.
