# Issue: gig drivers who work 8 to 12 hours inside their own car: the right leg and hip that never rest, and nowhere to pee at night

*Researched 2026-10-03 by buyer-researcher (job issues-niche-a-2026-10-03). Every quote has a link and a date; unknown stays unknown.*

**Counts: 33 quotes, 2 sites (UberPeople.net 15, YouTube comments 18), posted Oct 2024 to Sep 2026 (YouTube ages are relative, see the legend under section 9).** A third site could not be reached (see "Sites tried"). Group: US food-delivery, Amazon Flex and rideshare drivers (Uber, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Lyft). Two pains: **A** the body (right hip, thigh, knee, back) and **B** no safe place to use the bathroom, especially at night. Pain A has a product gap; pain B's products already exist and only the angle is open.

## 1. The issue in one line

"Lately, I've been having pain on my right inner thigh and right knee from pressing the break and gas pedal all day." (#3, a DoorDash and Uber Eats driver, 12 hours a day, six days a week). Pain B in their words: "I would like to know where ladies use the bathroom." (#20, an overnight driver).

## 2. What it feels like

- **Stuck, tried everything:** "I still did not find a fix and it is driving me crazy since I also do Uber Eats. I am trying anything. Seat extender, cushion pads, pedal extender." (#2)
- **Overlooked (nobody makes it for them):** "I’m a delivery driver and i couldn’t find any videos on stretches for delivery drivers." (#4). "Hi. What would you recommend for delivery drivers?" (#10, asked under a seat-cushion review video).
- **Worn down by the seat itself:** "I started Uber and my right knee was killing me" (#5). "I just started driving for Uber and Lyft, just a month ago and my lower back is killing me." (#6)
- **Exposed and unsafe (bathroom):** "I'm looking for a place that's clean and safe." (#21). "the ceiling light will not go out" so she squats behind the open back door (#22).
- **Improvising and a bit ashamed:** "Anyone miss the Gatorade bottle. I did before. Omg on my pants..haha." (#25). "I still swear by the pee-nut jar I keep in my glove box." (#26)

**Why it's underserved** (Amazon search pages fetched 2026-10-03; counts are Amazon's own "bought in past month" badges; TikTok Shop and Google were not reachable by fetch, so TikTok/ads/Google are **not checked**):
- **Seat cushions are crowded:** "wedge seat cushion for driving hip pain" shows 10-plus cushions with 200 to 4K+ bought a month; "uber driver seat cushion hip right side pain" shows ComfiLife, Everlasting Comfort and BlissTrends at 10K+ bought a month. None of the titles I read mention delivery, rideshare or one-sided pain; many pitch "sciatica" or "tailbone", which is a claim we cannot copy. Do not propose another cushion; the only gap is the "durable, does not flatten" angle (#14, #16), and that is a durability claim, not a new product.
- **Right-leg pads exist only as interior trim:** about 9 results at $6.99 to $27.99 with 50 to 100+ bought a month, named "Car Knee Cushion Pad", "Alcantane Style Car Leg Knee Pads, Black Line Right Leg Pad", sold on leather and style. Nothing is pitched at drivers who sit all shift.
- **Under-thigh extenders:** about 9 results, $29 to $56, mostly 50+ bought a month, low volume.
- **Dead-pedal and left-foot items** are truck or Jeep trim ($13 to $60) or disability accelerators ($110 to $161): not the same job.
- **"uber driver essentials kit"** returns first-aid kits, rating signs, vomit bags and back-seat charging boxes: nothing for the body on the shift. The "10 items all Uber drivers should have" video (YouTube, ~200K views) was read for comments only; its comments name a partition shield and a can of Lysol, not anything for comfort.
- **Bathroom items are saturated:** female urination devices $7.99 to $27.50 (up to 3K+/mo), pee bottles $8.75 to $9.98 (up to 10K+/mo), disposable urine bags (6K+/mo), portable toilets (5K+/mo). Only the "car urinal" titles hint at cars; none speak to women who drive at night. This is an angle, not a product gap.

**Weak spots and evidence against (read before spending anything):**
- Pain A has no quote that asks for a "leg rest pad". The pad is my inference from "right inner thigh and right knee" (#3) and "less space to rest the legs" (#2); test it before building anything.
- The right-leg pain comes from YouTube comments under videos by chiropractors and physios; UberPeople users mostly talk about the seat pad and walking. Only five posters say "right" at all (#1, #2, #3, #5, #13).
- UberPeople readers are price-sensitive and sceptical (in the site's Feb 13, 2025 snack-and-water thread most replies refuse to spend on extras); the $50 and $60 pads in #15 and #17 are the price anchors.
- Pain B: "This is why being a food delivery driver is better, because most restaurants have a bathroom available when you pickup." (#32, 177 likes). Food-delivery drivers are better off; the strongest need is night rideshare and Amazon Flex or van drivers, and a big share of the Amazon-bathroom video's comments are about company-van drivers, not own-car gig drivers. Of the YouTube bathroom quotes I kept, only #28 (Flex), #27 (Uber) and #29 (delivery, vehicle not stated) are clearly own-car.
- YouTube "1 year ago" really means 12 to 23 months, so those comments are dated Oct 2024 to Oct 2025 at best-guess; none can be pinned to a month.
- Nothing from the group's own TikTok comments was readable; this would be the best next source.
