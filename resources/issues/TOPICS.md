# Our topics: the issues we research, and the products they lead to

**Start here: [the desire map (DESIRES.md)](DESIRES.md)**, the 8 strongest everyday desires (rested, unwound, in control, focused, present, keep doing what I love, independent, confident), each tied to issues, groups and product types to search. Refreshed monthly.

**The feeling behind everything we sell** (owner, October 1): you want to do something and be great at it, but life throws you curveballs because you're so exhausted. As kids we had unlimited energy; now we don't.

**The rule** (owner): "find the problems that truly exist and direct them to us to help them. that's all it is." A small avatar group with a real problem is enough, because Meta finds those people through the ad. Every issue is researched with [the method](../../workflow/CHECKS.md), Step 1a: Gemini Deep Research (3.8 Flash) first, then agents verify and fill [the issue template](../../templates/ISSUE.md).

**Never:** supplements or anything eaten, devices that claim to treat a condition, or copy that says or implies the reader has a condition.

## How an issue turns into a product to market (owner, October 3)
1. **The issue list** (this page, always growing): Gemini Deep Research finds candidate issues for many avatars; agents cross-reference every claim against real posts and fill the issue file until it passes I1–I5 ([the method](../../workflow/CHECKS.md#the-issue-list-the-hard-part-done-first)).
2. **The owner picks a couple to test** on the Issues page.
3. **Product** (Step 1): a round per tested issue. The scout searches TikTok Shop for the issue's product types and "what fails them today", and keeps only products with real, stable or rising numbers (F1–F6).
4. **Buyers** (Step 2): confirm the product's own buyers match the issue's avatars, and add new quotes back here.
5. **Prove it** (Step 3): room on Meta for that avatar and angle, the exact item, the money line, a sample. Then Create, Build, Launch, Learn.

## Research order
One Gemini run at a time. New groups first (workers, hobbies, life stages and places not yet on the map), then deepen issues that missed I1 (truck drivers and gig drivers need a third site). The owner can reorder or add groups anytime.
