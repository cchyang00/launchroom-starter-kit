## Counter-evidence (against passing) and limits

- **The audience is being bought, with other problems.** Persona advertorials have run for 4 to 6 months, which tells us flight attendants respond to "I'm a flight attendant" voices. Our room and day-sleep angle is not on the page, but a copycat can arrive fast.
- **groundingwell.com is the one to watch.** Same story shape as spnutrition and a hotel-room line in the text; it turns 30 days on Oct 9. If it counts as speaking to the moment (it does mention five or six hours in a hotel room), the strict count becomes 3.
- **A sleep kit sells on TikTok but is thin on Meta.** I found zero Meta ads for a day-sleeper or hotel kit, but `portable blackout curtain` returns only 1 ad and an exact-phrase check of `crew` words is narrow; a seller using "traveler" or "night worker" words would not show here.
- The nearest day-sleeper product ad (vionb.com, a night worker, 23 d) says the same thing the crew say ("the doorbell was the enemy"); it points to the **night-shift** group, not crew.
- Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads; large all-words queries were read to a third or less (155 of ~160, 164 of ~2,000, 138 of ~830, 187 of ~7,100).
- Pilots: nothing at all on Meta for pilots (seat cushion, sleep). The pilot back avatar has no advertisers.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: PASS, narrow.** Strict count of advertisers speaking to the layover or hotel-rest moment with ads 30+ days old: **2** (spnutrition-us.com and purple.com). None sells the kit. A third (groundingwell.com) crosses 30 days on **Oct 9**; recheck then. Counting persona ads would fail the issue.
