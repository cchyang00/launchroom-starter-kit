# Issue: "pain between my shoulders and spine" when I practise (adult violin and viola players)

*One file per issue: `resources/issues/musicians/ISSUE.md`. Method and gate: [workflow/CHECKS.md](../../../workflow/CHECKS.md), Step 1. Researched 2026-10-03 by buyer-researcher (job issues-niche-b-musicians, set B, life stages and hobbies). Every quote has a link and a date; unknown stays unknown. Owner direction (Oct 3): non-saturated audiences and pain points that are intriguing and not something everyone has.*

**Group chosen and why.** Adult amateur violin and viola players: returners, late starters, community-orchestra and fiddle-circle players. Of the four instrument families (bowed strings, guitar and bass, drums, piano), only bowed strings could be read with real in-window volume: **violinist.com** alone had about 25 threads dated Oct 2024 to Sep 2026 on chin rests, shoulder rests, neck, collarbone, back and sweat, and **Fiddlerman** and **Fiddle Hangout** added more. The violin is also the one instrument where the body-to-instrument gap (jaw, collarbone, shoulder) must be filled by loose accessories the player assembles alone, so the pain and the fixes are both very specific. Drummer, bass and piano sites were behind challenge pages (see limits).

**Honest limits of this pass (read first):**
- **Read as raw page text and script-checked:** violinist.com (851 threads fetched, Sep 2024 to Oct 2026), the Fiddlerman forum, Fiddle Hangout. Every quote in section 9 was matched word for word against the saved page and its post date checked to be 3 Oct 2024 or later (34 of 34 kept passed; 1 candidate failed the match and was dropped, and a few more were left out as off-group, such as a parent's note about a child or a stranger's medical detail).
- **Not read:** Reddit, Quora, TikTok (search page is a JavaScript shell with no results), Amazon (HTTP 503 error page to an automated read, 2026-10-03). Drum, bass and piano forums (drumforum.org, talkbass.com, pianoworld.com and others) were already known to sit behind challenge pages and were not bypassed. Hacker News and the Lemmy network were searched and held nothing on this topic.
- **No Amazon or TikTok Shop listings were seen**, so "what is on sale there" below comes from two violin shops' public product feeds (Fiddlershop, Shar Music, read 2026-10-03), not from the marketplaces. Someone must run the search terms in section 6 in a signed-in browser. Re-gate, 2026-10-03: TikTok Shop's public search pages load without a login and were read for the I4 check (section 6); Amazon is still unseen.
- Quotes keep the writers' typos and punctuation. No usernames are copied. Quotes are for learning their words, never for fake reviews.

## 1. The issue in one line

The aches come a few minutes into practice, and the chin rest and shoulder rest they own don't quite fit their body or their room, so they patch the gap with cloths, foam, cork and tape (our summary of the quotes below). In one player's words: "When I play/practice I get pain between my shoulders and spine, especially on the left side." (violinist.com, 10 Jul 2025)

## 2. What it feels like

- **An ache that arrives on schedule:** "The ache starts to come on after playing a few minutes and disappears when I take my violin down off my shoulder." (Fiddlerman, 8 Dec 2024)
- **Rationing practice to the ache:** "I only play for a few minutes at a time, then I stretch or use a heating pad. That only gives temporary relief though." (violinist.com, 11 Jul 2025)
- **Months of it, with no clear cause:** "I’ve been struggling for months now with a couple of aches and pains ! Mostly in my neck and trapezius areas." (Fiddlerman, 8 Dec 2024)
- **Dread of the skin changing:** "I’m a recreational player and usually play 1-2 hours 5-6 days a week. I changed my chin rest a few years ago and developed a small thickening of the skin under the jaw but didn’t think anything of it. A couple of days ago, I was surprised to find that this area was noticeably larger and tender to touch." (violinist.com, 19 Dec 2025)
- **Hot, sweaty and cornered by heat:** "We are in the midst of a heat wave again here in the UK, and practise is awful because of how sweaty I get." (violinist.com, 15 Aug 2026)
- **Fed up with the pile of gear:** "I myself also have a huge neck, and I use no shoulder rest but a very tall chinrest, 5 cm or so. I don't really like stuffing a bunch of crud under the violin." (violinist.com, 21 Dec 2024)

### The moment and its cost (I2, checked 2026-10-03)

**The moment that keeps coming back:** a few minutes into practice the ache starts, and it goes the moment the violin comes off the shoulder; it repeats every session. "The ache starts to come on after playing a few minutes and disappears when I take my violin down off my shoulder." (#25, Fiddlerman, 8 Dec 2024)
**What it costs, in their words:** practice time, "I only play for a few minutes at a time, then I stretch or use a heating pad. That only gives temporary relief though." (#2, violinist.com, 11 Jul 2025); money while it is unsolved, "I’m on hold about the Wave chinrest for now…..$75 is a lot of money." ## 7. Angles per avatar

One native-ad situation per avatar, in situation language. Never "you have X".

- **A:** "For the adult who picked the violin back up, plays after work, and notices the ache starts a few minutes in and eases the moment the violin comes off the shoulder."

**Why a native ad on this moment stops the scroll:** the group is tiny and has no ad aimed at it, and the details are ones outsiders never see: the two-inch gap between jaw and violin, the cloth that deadens the tone, the foam that smears a good shirt, the practice that only lasts a few minutes before the ache. A player who sees their own August practice room described exactly will stop, because no mass-market ad has ever mentioned a chin rest.
