## Counter-evidence and limits

- **The hand-ache lane has paid back for four months.** Hand Hearth has run since May 30 and resilea.store copied its wording within 3 months; that is the pattern of a working angle. These sellers claim relief for thumb arthritis and use compression and heat, which we cannot copy.
- The back and shoulder lane is open, but the buyer evidence is thin: ISSUE.md has the lap pillow on one forum mention only, and the tilt platform at "modest evidence". An empty Meta lane here may mean few people want it on Meta.
- The all-words queries were sorted by impressions and read to roughly 200 of up to ~2,000 reported; a small seller outside the top by impressions would not show. Meta's text search cannot hear speech in video ads.
- Margaret Whitmore's product could not be named from the text read; it is counted because the ad speaks directly to a knitter's thumb.

## Gate result

**I7 · Not owned on Meta: FAIL (hands and wrists).** 5 advertisers with ads 30+ days old speak to knitters' and quilters' hand strain. If this issue is tested, use the open back, shoulder and arm-support lane (tilt platform, lap and arm pillow: 0 to 1 advertisers) and re-run I7 on that wording first. The full product-level check stays Prove it P1.
