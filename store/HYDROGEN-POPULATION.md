# Populate the Hydrogen Storefront (`base-theme.json` -> `<slug>.json`)

**Updated November 5, 2026.** Local Hydrogen preview runs on **`http://localhost:3021`**; Launchroom OS runs on **`http://localhost:3000/p/merrivy/`**.

---

## 1. Clone from the 10/10 Master Base Theme (`base-theme.json`) + 52 Color Palettes

Owner (November 3): *"what i love is what we have already 100%. it just needs to be a base theme with a dummy product and make it 10/10 with a bunch of different color palettes... that way, the agents just basically copy from that base build, and then build the product pages easily from that base build."*

1. **Start Every New Product by Cloning `base-theme.json`:**
   - Inside `hydrogen-storefront/`, run:
     ```bash
     node scripts/packet-tools.mjs new <slug> --palette <palette-id> --live
     ```
   - Or clone directly from the **Store & Theme Hub (`#/store`)** in Launchroom.
   - **Never overwrite or delete `app/content/base-theme.json`!** `app/content/templates.ts` uses `base-theme.json` as its default fallback so that when a failed product is wiped via `Cancel & Wipe Product` in `#/manage`, MiniOxygen (`http://localhost:3021`) never crashes.
   - **A sample packet shows only in local previews.** On the connected store, a page whose own packet has `"status": "sample"` shows the "Private store setup" screen instead, so demo claims never reach shoppers. Before going live, make sure the product's packet isn't `sample`. Local previews never load the Meta pixel or WeTracked, so they can't add junk visits.
2. **Use `witching-hour-planner.json` as the 10/10 Visual & Copy Benchmark:**
   - Every product packet in `hydrogen-storefront/app/content/<slug>.json` inherits the exact 5-section Grimoire layout (`"sections": ["features", "comparison", "quotes", "guarantee", "faq"]`) + 3 vertical creator demo videos (`demonstrations`) + `.is-wftn-reviews` verified customer review cards.
3. **52 WCAG-Verified Color Palettes (`app/content/palettes.json`):**
   - Pick the palette in `app/content/palettes.json` that matches the product's mood and season (e.g., Warm Parchment & Espresso for the Grimoire Planner; Solar Amber & Slate for emergency hardware). All colors come from theme tokens.

## 2. Configure the 2-Kit Offer + `$4.99` Shipping Protection (`catalog`, `purchase`, `offer`)

Owner (November 4): *"On the checklist remember, we're not going with the 3-kit anymore so it's only 2 kits, okay? We're not going with bundles anymore. It just becomes more of a headache dealing with add-ons. But yeah all the upsell stuff should be on the checklist for sure: Flawless mobile design, Strong high-converting copy angles, Making sure the shipping, the `$4.99`s, are on there correctly."*

1. **Strictly 2 Kits in `purchase.offerChoices` and `catalog.variants`:**
   - **1-Kit (Standard):** Base retail price (`$P`) + **`$7.99` Standard Shipping** (`3–5` Business Days).
   - **2-Kit (`MOST POPULAR · SAVE 15%`):** `2` units at `15%` off (`round(2 * P * 0.85) - 0.01`, with `compareAtPrice: 2 * P`) + **FREE Tracked Shipping**.
   - **Never add a 3-Kit / 3-Pack option.**
2. **Cart-Drawer-Only Upsells (`$4.99` Shipping Protection + 1-Tap 2-Kit Upgrade):**
   - Set `offer.shippingProtection` to **`$4.99`** (Priority Insured Delivery & Package Protection toggle inside the slide-out Cart Drawer).
   - Keep `BagAddOns` empty (`[]`)—do not create multi-SKU physical companion add-on bundles.
   - When a shopper adds the `1-Kit` to the bag, the slide-out Cart Drawer displays the 1-tap **`Upgrade to 2-Kit (SAVE 15% + FREE Tracked Shipping)`** banner.

## 3. Website vs. Meta Ad Headline Split & Image Slots

1. **Elevated Boutique/Hardware Titles on the Website:**

   - Put elevated boutique/hardware titles in `hero.headline` and `purchase.heading`, and weave top viral creator reveal language into `purchase.hook` and the 3 `demonstrations.items[]` video cards.
