> **Core Doctrine — Winning Statics Come From Winning Viral Videos:**
> Never invent random static ideas from scratch. First, transcribe and deconstruct the product's top viral TikTok videos (spoken hook, mechanism numbers like *"5-step filtration down to 0.1 micron"*, and visual demonstration) + competitor research + 4–5 star Amazon reviews. Then map those exact findings into a **20-Static Ad Batch (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`)** using the reference pools below and generate every image inside **Google Flow (`https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow`)** in the owner's signed-in Chrome browser.

## FLAGS LEGEND

- **`LOC`** = Localization elements present (spelling, currency, country names, seasonal window, or regulator badge).
- **`REALISM`** = Human-realism protocol ON (photorealistic candid skin texture, pores, ambient lighting, identity lock across panels, before-state protection).
- **`STRUCT-ONLY`** = Replicate layout structure only; strip borrowed authority (press logos, named endorsers, competitor trademarks -> genericize or replace with our verified proof).
- **`CLAIM`** = Aggressive transformation/health/durability claim structure; ensure compliance with Meta Personal Attributes policy (`C3` / `claims-rights`) and include the standard one-line platform note in the batch summary.
- **`VARIANT-OF`** = Near-duplicate layout kept in the 50-static catalog (`static_26` is a variant of `static_10`); never use both in the same batch.

## FAMILY DEFINITIONS (`F1–F9`)

### F1 — Photographic Before/After
Real-photo transformation. Mechanism: credibility through unretouched
realism. The imperfection IS the persuasion. REALISM always ON.
QC: identity lock between panels (same person, angle, lighting env),
before-state protection (model must not beautify the "before").

### F2 — Illustrated Before/After & Mechanism Diagrams
Drawn/3D condition contrast or how-it-works anatomy. Mechanism: education
+ self-diagnosis ("that's what's happening inside me"). Sidesteps photo
credibility problems. REALISM OFF — stylization is the point.

### F3 — Annotated Product Callouts
Product hero + labeled callout lines to features/benefits. Mechanism:
density of proof for solution-aware comparers. Preserve: callout geometry,
trust bar. Swap: product, labels, colors.

### F4 — Us-vs-Them Comparison
Two-column tables, side-by-sides, "ordinary vs ours," ranked tests.
Mechanism: framing the choice so the product wins on the user's criteria.
Most-aware/solution-aware. Highly swappable, low realism risk.

### F5 — Testimonial & Social-Proof Styles
Quote cards, post-it handwritten notes, review-UI frames, person-holding-
product portraits, AMA/story-style. Mechanism: peer proof. NOTE: use
real verified buyer quotes from our 4–5 star Amazon / TikTok Shop research
(attributed to our active Avatar Persona POV such as `Olivia Scott — Verified Buyer`
or `Michael L — Verified Buyer`) — never invent unsubstantiated claims.

### F6 — Big-Claim Product Hero
Dominant headline claim + clean product presentation, often with offer
bar or stat strip. The workhorse family. Mechanism: single-claim clarity.

### F7 — Editorial/Advertorial Style
Article-look framing, serif long headlines, story logic, authority notes.
Mechanism: borrowed context of journalism/expertise. THIN FAMILY (2 refs):
expect less structural variety per user request; disclose when users ask
for large all-advertorial batches.

### F8 — Problem-Agitation Visual
Pain-point imagery dominant; product secondary or ABSENT. Anatomy shots,
exposé framing, pattern-interrupt photography. Mechanism: press the
problem until the click relieves it. Product-optional confirmed.

### F9 — Grid, Checklist & Catalog Formats
Multi-panel avatar grids, product grids, icon/benefit checklists,
seasonal posters. Mechanism: breadth-of-fit ("one of these is me").

## SELECTION & ADAPTATION RULES

1. **20-Static Viral-Video Split (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`):**
   - Select **10 references from Batch A (`F8`, `F2`, `F7`, `F1`)** to dramatize the hidden problem, scary domino chain, or internal mechanism revealed in the product's viral videos.
   - Select **10 references from Batch B (`F4`, `F3`, `F5`, `F6`, `F9`)** to prove our product's superiority over competitors/ordinary alternatives using the viral video's mechanism proof + 4–5 star Amazon reviews + our 2-Kit offer.
2. **Never place two `VARIANT-OF` siblings (`static_10` and `static_26`) in one batch.**
3. **Weight down `WEAK SIGNAL` refs (`static_20`)** unless specifically requested for a multi-model collage.
4. **`REALISM` flags inject the human-realism protocol block:** natural skin texture with visible pores, imperfect ambient lighting, no plastic AI sheen, before-state protection, and identity lock across panels.
5. **`LOC` flags trigger the market-swap checklist:** localize spelling, currency, and season for our target market (`US / CA / UK / AU`); never invent a regulator badge or certification—swap to our verified review score or 30-day guarantee.
6. **`STRUCT-ONLY` refs:** replicate the visual skeleton only; strip borrowed authority (press logos, named doctors/endorsers, competitor trademarks -> genericize or replace with our verified buyer proof).
7. **Strip captured platform UI chrome:** Source PNGs like `static_12` and `static_24` include captured ad-interface elements (destination URLs, Shop Now buttons, screenshot borders) outside the creative. Replicate only the intended creative composition inside the frame.
