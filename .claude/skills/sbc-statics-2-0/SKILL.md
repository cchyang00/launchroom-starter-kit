# `sbc-statics-2.0` — Viral-Mechanism Concept Ledger, Direct Image Copy & 5 Evergreen Captions + 5 Headlines

Invocation: `$sbc-statics-2-0`. Paired with [winning-statics](../winning-statics/SKILL.md), [viral-hook-and-dr-copywriting](../viral-hook-and-dr-copywriting/SKILL.md), and [google-flow-creative-studio](../google-flow-creative-studio/SKILL.md).

This skill governs **how we turn a product's winning viral TikTok videos, competitor research, and 4–5 star Amazon reviews into a semantically diverse 20-Static Ad Batch (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`)**, plus **five substantial evergreen Facebook captions (`250–400 words each`) and five short reusable headlines (`4–9 words each`)** delivered automatically with every batch.

---

## 1. Collect & Deconstruct the Viral-Video + Research Brief First

Read the product's research packet (`research/<round>/L###.md`, `research/<round>/buyers/L###.md`), downloaded viral TikTok videos (`kalodata_top20_manifest.json` / `.mp4` transcripts & frames), and storefront packet (`hydrogen-storefront/app/content/<slug>.json`) first. Never carry brand facts from a different product into a new one.

Establish these five core inputs in `products/P###-slug/creative/COPY-BRIEF.md`:

1. **Viral Video Deconstruction (Why the Viral Videos Hit):**
   - What exact **spoken hook**, **mechanism explanation**, **specific numbers** (e.g., *"5-step filtration process that removes glass shards and minerals down to 0.1 micron"*), and **visual proof** made the top TikTok videos go viral?
2. **Product Truth & Offer (`1-Kit` vs `2-Kit`):**
   - Exact hardware/material specs, dimensions, what arrives in the box, `1-Kit` base price vs. `2-Kit` (`★ MOST POPULAR · BEST VALUE`, `Save 15%` + `FREE Tracked Shipping`), `30-Day Money-Back Guarantee`, and `$4.99` Shipping Protection in the cart drawer. Never invent unverified discounts, gifts, or 3-Kit bundles.
3. **Target Avatar(s) + Concrete Moments:**
   - Who is this for? What specific physical situation or crisis domino chain are they in? What happens, where does it happen, what do they do, and what ordinary, observable change do they want?
   - Which Avatar Persona Page voices the campaign (`Olivia Scott` for female-skewed/lifestyle/comfort vs. `Michael L` for male/homeowner/preparedness/hardware)?
4. **20-Static Awareness Split (`10 Problem-Aware / Unaware` + `10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Superiority`):**
   - **Batch A (`10 Problem-Aware / Unaware`):** Exposes a scary hidden problem or urgent crisis domino chain that cold scrollers didn't know existed (pulled from the viral video's problem hook).
   - **Batch B (`10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Superiority`):** Proves why our product's engineering and 4–5 star buyer-praised features beat ordinary store-bought alternatives and cheap copycats.
5. **The Causal Mechanism Chain (Mandatory for Every Claim):**
   - Explain the product strictly as:
     > **`feature / filtration stage / component (from viral video) -> what it actually does -> practical benefit -> why that matters in this person's day`**
   - Include only links supported by the viral video demonstration, verified supplier specs, and 4–5 star reviews. Never manufacture clinical claims or pad weak evidence with jargon.

## 2. Design Diversity Before Generating: The 20-Static Concept Ledger

Before generating a single image in Google Flow, create a **20-row Concept Ledger** (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`) mapped to `references/concept-library.md` (`static_01.png`–`static_50.png`):

| Ad ID | Batch (`A: Problem` / `B: Solution`) | Ref Layout (`static_XX`) & Family (`F1–F9`) | Viral Video / Competitor Hook Source | Concrete Moment & Emotional Angle | On-Image Headline (`3–7` Words) | Callout / Sub-Copy Strings (Verbatim) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Diversity Audit Rules (Zero Lazy Clones):
- **Each row must have both a distinct meaning AND a distinct visual structure.**
- Recoloring a background, swapping a person's shirt, moving a product bottle 2 inches, or using a synonym does **not** create a new concept.
- Identify and replace the nearest semantic or structural duplicates **before** rendering in Google Flow:
  - Across **Batch A (`10 Problem-Aware`):** Vary across hidden contamination/danger exposés (`F8`), step-by-step internal mechanism diagrams (`F2`), insider notebook/editorial warnings (`F7`), and crisis domino-chain arcs (`F1`).
  - Across **Batch B (`10 Solution-Aware`):** Vary across Us-vs-Them durability/scam comparisons (`F4`), multi-point engineering callouts (`F3`), handwritten Post-It & IG Story AMA peer proof (`F5`), and high-contrast hero/avatar checklist layouts (`F6`/`F9`).
- Never use both `VARIANT-OF` siblings (`static_10` and `static_26`) in the same batch.

## 3. Direct Image Copy Rules (By Awareness Level)

1. **Make the Audience & Specific Problem/Desire Recognizable in `<0.8` Seconds:**
   - Give each static ad **one main idea**. The top headline (`3–7` words where layout permits) must express a complete thought alone—never rely on the Facebook caption to make sense of an incomplete image headline.
2. **Match Copy Directly to the Batch's Awareness Level:**
   - **Batch A (`10 Problem-Aware / Unaware`):** Introduce an overlooked threat, hidden flaw in everyday habits, or concrete physical domino chain (*"First the bottled water is gone -> then the power cuts out -> then tap pressure drops -> What will you do?"*; *"What's actually floating inside clear municipal tap water?"*). Do not force a hard bundle discount pitch onto an unaware problem-agitation graphic (`static_09`, `static_22`, `static_31`).
   - **Batch B (`10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Superiority`):** Explain the specific mechanism revealed in the viral video (*"5-stage 0.1-micron filtration vs. ordinary carbon pitchers"*, *"16.4ft split-panel outdoor charge + indoor ceiling light vs. fumes from gas generators"*), resolve practical buyer objections from 2–4 star reviews, and highlight the 2-Kit Free Shipping offer where appropriate.
3. **Concrete Words Over Vague Marketing Filler:**
   - Replace vague words like *"support," "optimization," "wellness,"* or *"elevate"* with concrete physical actions, numbers from the viral video, and observable everyday outcomes.
4. **Authentic Evidence Only:**
   - Use only real review quotes from our 4–5 star Amazon/TikTok research (attributed to our Avatar Persona POV `Olivia Scott — Verified Buyer` or `Michael L — Verified Buyer`), verified viral-video demonstrations, and our true 2-Kit offer. Never invent press logos, doctor endorsements, or regulator badges.

## 4. Deliver 5 Long-Form Evergreen Facebook Captions (`250–400 Words`) + 5 Short Headlines (`4–9 Words`)

Every completed static ad batch **must** be accompanied by **five distinct, substantial evergreen Facebook captions** and **five short reusable headlines** saved in `products/P###-slug/creative/COPY-BRIEF.md` (and `captions.txt` / `headlines.txt` in the creative folder) and synced into `os/data/meta-campaigns.json`.

### A. Five Long-Form Evergreen Facebook Captions (`250–400 Words Each`)
Write **exactly 5 distinct long-form captions** (`250–400 words` each) voiced from our active Avatar Persona Page (`Olivia Scott` or `Michael L`). Every caption must:
1. **Qualify the audience in the first 2 lines** using either a viral-video reveal hook or a concrete physical domino chain ending in a self-interrogation question (*"What will you do?"*).
2. **Mirror a real-life trigger moment** from our buyer research (what happened, where they were, why ordinary alternatives failed them).
3. **Develop the emotional & practical cost** of leaving the problem unsolved.
4. **Introduce the product & walk through the Causal Mechanism Chain** pulled from the winning viral video (`feature/stage -> what it actually does -> practical benefit -> why it matters in their day`).
5. **Include credible social proof** (real 4–5 star buyer observations and why thousands switched from generic competitors).
6. **Connect to the everyday transformation** (relief, preparedness, pride, peace of mind).
7. **State the exact 2-Kit offer clearly** (`1-Kit` single option or `2-Kit` `★ MOST POPULAR · BEST VALUE` with `15% OFF` + `FREE Tracked Shipping` + `30-Day Guarantee`) and end with a clear, urgent CTA (*"Tap Shop Now below to grab yours before this batch sells out!"*).

**Vary the Narrative Structure Across All 5 Captions:**
- **Caption 1 — The Viral Video Mechanism Reveal Story:** Opens with the exact counter-intuitive demonstration/numbers from the top viral video (*"When I saw the video cutting open a standard filter vs. this 5-stage 0.1-micron cartridge..."*).
- **Caption 2 — The Unaware Domino-Chain Wake-Up Call:** Walks through the concrete step-by-step escalation of the problem and forces *"What will you do?"* before introducing the hardware solution.
- **Caption 3 — The Sceptic-Turned-Believer / Spouse Conversion Story:** First-person confession (*"My husband laughed when I ordered the 2-Kit... until Tuesday night when..."*).
- **Caption 4 — The Us-vs-Them Competitor Exposé:** Breaks down why 4 out of 5 ordinary store-bought options fail after 30 days and the 3 engineering details that make this one last.
- **Caption 5 — The Everyday Ritual & Objection-Crusher Story:** Answers the top 3 hesitant buyer questions (setup time, durability, real-world output) through a warm day-in-the-life narrative.

*Formatting Rules for Captions:* Use short, scannable 1–3 sentence paragraphs and purposeful emojis. Never include raw URLs inside the body text, research citations, batch-record notes, or Meta-banned personal attributes language (*"Do you suffer from..."*).

### B. Five Short Reusable Meta Headlines (`4–9 Words Each`)
Write **exactly 5 distinct short headlines** (`4–9 words` each) designed as a reusable pool that pairs cleanly with any of the 20 statics or 1–3 viral videos:
- **Headline 1 (Viral Reveal + Urgency):** e.g., `"You Have to See How This Works — Get Yours Today!"`
- **Headline 2 (Problem / Crisis Trigger):** e.g., `"When the Grid Goes Dark, What Will You Do?"`
- **Headline 3 (Mechanism / Number Proof):** e.g., `"5-Stage 0.1-Micron Filtration In Your Palm"`
- **Headline 4 (Competitor Superiority / Objection Crusher):** e.g., `"Why 14,000+ Homeowners Replaced Cheap Store Filters"`
- **Headline 5 (2-Kit Offer + Free Shipping):** e.g., `"Grab the 2-Kit Today & Get FREE Tracked Shipping!"`

## 5. Pre-Delivery Batch Audit Checklist

Before marking the creative job complete:
- [ ] **Viral Video Grounding:** Every static angle and mechanism claim traces back to the product's winning viral video transcript/frames, competitor research, and 4–5 star reviews.
- [ ] **20-Static Split Verified:** Exactly `10 Problem-Aware / Unaware` statics (Batch A) + `10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Superiority` statics (Batch B) generated in Google Flow (`https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow`), each using a distinct `static_01..50.png` reference layout + real product truth photo.
- [ ] **Visual & Mobile-Feed QA:** All 20 images inspected at full resolution—exact product geometry, zero fake logos, zero garbled/clipped text, `>= 6%` edge margins.
- [ ] **5 Captions + 5 Headlines Complete:** All 5 long-form evergreen captions (`250–400 words`) and 5 short headlines (`4–9 words`) written, compliance-checked (`C3` / `claims-rights`), and saved alongside the batch.
