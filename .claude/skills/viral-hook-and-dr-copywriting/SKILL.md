## 1. Step 0 — Deconstruct Winning Viral Videos, Competitor Research & 4–5 Star Reviews First

Never invent copy or static ad ideas from scratch. Every copywriting job starts by extracting three verified inputs into `products/P###-slug/creative/COPY-BRIEF.md` ([templates/COPY-BRIEF.md](../../../templates/COPY-BRIEF.md)):

1. **Top Viral Creator Video Transcripts, Mechanism Numbers & Visual Demos (`kalodata_top20_manifest.json` / `.mp4` saves):**
   - Listen to and transcribe the **exact 3-second spoken opening hooks**, **the core mechanism explanation & numbers** (e.g., `"There's a 5-step filtration process that removes glass shards and minerals down to 0.1 micron"`, `"You see this? This is 100% free electricity right here!"`), and **"best-light reveal" captions** from the top revenue-generating US TikTok videos.
   - Build the **`sbc-statics-2-0` Causal Chain**: `feature / filtration stage (from viral video) -> what it actually does -> practical benefit -> why that matters in this person's day`.
2. **Top 3 Buyer-Praised Features & Competitor Weaknesses (`research/<round>/buyers/L###.md`):**
   - From **4–5 star Amazon reviews**, extract the **top 3 specific features buyers rave about** (and the exact trigger moment that made them buy).
   - From **2–4 star reviews, TikTok comments & competitor ads**, extract why ordinary alternatives or cheap knockoffs fail, plus the top 4–6 practical buyer hesitations (sizing, setup, brightness/runtime, shipping time, durability).
3. **20-Static Concept Ledger (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`) + `5` Evergreen Captions & `5` Headlines (`sbc-statics-2-0`):**
   - **Batch A (`10 Problem-Aware / Unaware Statics`):** Map the viral video's scary hidden problem or crisis trigger onto `F8` Problem-Agitation (`static_07`, `static_09`, `static_22`, `static_27`, `static_31`, `static_49`), `F2` Illustrated Mechanism Diagrams (`static_01`, `static_05`, `static_10`, `static_44`, `static_45`), `F7` Editorial Notes (`static_03`, `static_06`), and `F1` Before/After (`static_11`, `static_15`, `static_30`).
   - **Batch B (`10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Superiority Statics`):** Map the viral video's mechanism proof + competitor research + 4–5 star reviews onto `F4` Us-vs-Them (`static_04`, `static_24`, `static_33`, `static_38`, `static_41`, `static_48`), `F3` Annotated Callouts (`static_12`, `static_32`, `static_42`), `F5` Testimonials & Post-Its (`static_14`, `static_17`, `static_19`, `static_25`, `static_40`, `static_46`), and `F6`/`F9` Hero & Checklists (`static_02`, `static_08`, `static_18`, `static_28`, `static_34`, `static_35`, `static_39`, `static_47`, `static_50`).
   - Write **5 distinct long-form evergreen Facebook captions (`250–400 words each`)** and **5 short reusable headlines (`4–9 words each`)** per [sbc-statics-2-0](../sbc-statics-2-0/SKILL.md).

## 3. Hard Rule 2 — Unaware-Scroller Domino Chain -> `"What Will You Do?"` (`4:5` DR Static Ads)

Cold scrollers on Facebook and Instagram are **100% unaware** of our brand and are not actively shopping. To stop the scroll and trigger an immediate click:

1. **Never Use Vague Opening Lines:**
   - Do **not** write *"With everything happening in the government..."* or *"In uncertain times..."*—an unaware scroller asks *"Wait, what's happening?"* and keeps scrolling.
2. **State the Concrete Physical Domino Chain or Specific Viral-Video Fact First, Then Ask `"What Will You Do?"`:**
   - **For Preparedness / Hardware / Water / Home Protection Products (`P007`):** Walk through the concrete physical sequence of how fast normal life gets stripped away:
     > *"First the bottled water at the store is gone. Then the food shelves empty. Next the power cuts out, the gas pumps shut down, and the water stops running. When night falls on your street, what will you do?"*
     Or lead with the specific mechanism fact from the viral video (*"There's a 5-step filtration process that removes invisible glass shards, heavy metals, and microplastics down to 0.1 micron—what's actually sitting in your tap water right now?"*).
   - **For Everyday Routine / Sleep / Comfort / Journal Products (`P004`):** Turn a seemingly ordinary product into an urgent, visceral human feeling around **forgetting precious memories, an overwhelmed racing mind at night, or having nowhere safe to express who you really are**:
     > *"How much of this past year did you already forget—and what do you have right now to keep 2027 from blurring away too?"*
     > *"Every time I open this in a coffee shop, someone stops to ask where I found a 2027 planner that isn't corporate beige."* (`5.48%` Link CTR winner).
3. **Pair Every DR Static Ad Hook with `3–7` Words + Viral Mechanism / `3` Review-Proven Features:**
   - Top headline: **`3–7` words** (`<0.8s` scan time).
   - Visual body: the viral video's mechanism stages (`F2`/`F3`) or the **3 best features from 4–5 star reviews / 2-column Us-vs-Them comparison** (`F4`/`F6`) so the viewer is sold on the mechanism before clicking through.

## 4. Hard Rule 3 — Avatar Persona Pages & 1st-Person Buyer Voice

1. **Post From Relatable Avatar Persona Pages (Never the Store Logo Page `Merrivy`):**
   - **Female-Skewed / Lifestyle / Routine / Comfort Products:** Write in the 1st-person voice of **`Olivia Scott`** (`page_id: 506843939180283` / `Debra T`, `1074186642452660`).
   - **Male / Homeowner / Preparedness / Garage / Hardware Products:** Write in the 1st-person voice of **`Michael L`** (`page_id: 1092000083990611`).
2. **Sound Like a Person, Never a Store (`"I bought this..."`):**
   - A store's voice triggers ad blindness; a real buyer's 1st-person confession (*"I bought the 2-pack after our last 14-hour outage..."*, *"My husband laughed when I mounted the solar panel outside, until our whole street lost power Tuesday night..."*) reads like native feed content.

## 5. Hard Rule 4 — Strictly 2-Kit Offer Copy (`1-Kit` vs `2-Kit` + `$4.99` Shipping Protection)

When writing storefront packets (`hydrogen-storefront/app/content/<slug>.json`, [templates/PRODUCT-PAGE-COPY.md](../../../templates/PRODUCT-PAGE-COPY.md)) and ad CTAs:

1. **Strictly 2 Options on the Hero Selector (`purchase.offerChoices`):**
   - **Option 1 (`1-Kit` Anchor):** Clean base retail price (`$34.99–$54.99`). **NEVER write `+$7.99 Shipping` on the hero selector card**—keep the front-page selector friction-free; `$7.99` Standard Shipping appears only in the Cart Drawer and Checkout.
   - **Option 2 (`2-Kit` — `★ MOST POPULAR · BEST VALUE`):** `Save 15%` (`Buy 1, Get 2nd 30%–43% OFF` decoy math) + **`FREE Tracked Shipping`** badge. Keep the top ribbon concise (`★ MOST POPULAR · BEST VALUE`) so it fits on a single line at `375px` mobile width.
2. **Never Write 3-Kit or Companion Add-On Bundle Copy:**
   - Never include a 3-Kit / 3-Pack tier, and keep `addOnIds: []` / `BagAddOns: []`.
   - Write the Cart Drawer upsell copy strictly for the **1-Tap `1-Kit -> 2-Kit` Upgrade Banner** and the **`$4.99` Shipping & Transit Protection** toggle.
3. **Meta Compliance Scan (`C3` / `claims-rights`):**
   - Never write *"Do you suffer from..."*, *"Your insomnia/anxiety/pain"*, or medical treatment/cure claims (Meta Personal Attributes policy). Sell comfort, preparedness, routines, and craftsmanship.

## 6. Hard Rule 5 — High-Conviction Power-Word Lexicon & Idiom/Grammar Gate (Owner Rule, November 5, 2026)

> *"It shouldn't be 'turn any water into pure drinking water at one touch.' It should be 'with one touch.' And we need stronger words, like 'military grade.' 'One touch,' to me, doesn't feel as powerful. We need to do some deep research into some stronger terms and build that into our copywriting skill because right now we're lacking some copywriting skills because our words aren't strong enough."*

1. **Never Lead With Weak, Generic Consumer Words When Selling High-Ticket (`$100+`) or Preparedness/Specialty Products:**
   - Replace soft feature adjectives (*"One-Touch"*, *"Portable"*, *"Easy"*, *"Nice"*, *"Good"*, *"Convenient"*) with **High-Conviction Authority & Specification Power Words**:
     - **Preparedness / Filtration / Tactical / Hardware (`$100–$150+`):** `Military-Grade`, `Medical-Grade 0.01-Micron`, `Tactical`, `99.9999% Elimination`, `Grid-Down`, `Self-Priming Pressurized`, `Sub-Micron Barrier`, `Expedition-Spec`, `793-Gallon Reserve`, `Zero-Gravity / Zero-Suction`.
     - **Boutique / Grimoire / Heirloom / Home (`$35–$85+`):** `Heirloom`, `Archival 120gsm`, `Gold-Foil Embossed`, `Bespoke`, `Apothecary`, `Sanctuary`, `Hand-Bound`, `First-Printing Edition`.
2. **Mandatory Native-English Idiom & Grammar Gate (Before Any Image Prompt or Store Packet Saves):**
   - Check every preposition and idiom before passing text to Google Flow or saving `<slug>.json`:
     - **Say:** `"With One Touch"` — **NEVER say:** `"At One Touch"` (broken non-native preposition).
     - **Say:** `"In 60 Seconds"` / `"At the Press of a Button"` — **NEVER** mix them into awkward hybrids.
   - Read every baked-in image headline out loud before generating in Google Flow. If a phrase sounds robotic, translated, or grammatically off, rewrite it immediately.

## 7. Hard Rule 6 — The Sacred Oak Storefront Copywriting Arc: Features in the Hero -> "Why This Is For You" Below (Zero Repetition, Short Headers — Owner Rule, Nov 5)

Owner Rule (November 5, 2026):
> *"The images explain what the product does, but we're missing some text below. Because you're repeating things, because you're saying 'military-grade sixth-stage water purifier,' right, but you're making that twice. What we're missing is kind of what my brother has, where he has these little checks. So for his light product he has never run out of power: 25 hours of power on one charge. Stay connected in any blackout. My biggest complaint is that you have 'military grade' explained three times. That's such a waste of space... And then the weakest spot is the 'why 2080+ families and campers upgraded from gravity bags and suction straws.' That text is too long... Needs to have a header like 'Why This Is for You' or something like that. At the top in the hero section are the features. Slowly as we get to the bottom, we tell why it's made for them. That's why our sites are not converting: because we don't have that spot where it says why it's made for them. That's where we need good images. That's where we need to have better copywriting and not repeat the same things that we already explained because they already know about the features. Why are we explaining features again?"*

1. **Zero Triple Repetition in the Hero (`hero` + `purchase`):**
   - Never repeat the same phrase (`"Military-Grade 6-Stage 0.01-Micron..."`) three times across the hero image, eyebrow, H1 title, and variant card.
   - **H1 Title (`hero.headline`):** Short, clean product name (`4–6` words max, e.g., `"One-Touch Portable Electric Water Purifier"`). Never print `navLabel` above `hero.headline`.
   - **Urgency/Hook Paragraph (`hero.subhead`, `25–35` words max):** Right below the title, hit the core emergency/daily pain point and use `"military-grade"` **once** where it hits hardest:
     - *Example:* `"Don't wait until a boil-water alert or blackout leaves your family without drinking water. Get this military-grade 0.01-micron purifier now to turn any tap, creek, or rain barrel into pure drinking water with one touch."`
   - **3–4 Short Checkmark Bullets (`hero.benefits`, `4–7` words each with `✔` icons):**
     - *Sacred Oak Benchmark:* `✔ Never Run Out Of Power` · `✔ 25 Hours Of Light In 1 Charge` · `✔ Stay Connected In Any Blackout`
     - *Water Purifier Benchmark:* `✔ Pure Drinking Water With One Touch` · `✔ 793 Gallons Per Filter (6,000 Bottles)` · `✔ Zero Manual Pumping Or Straws` · `✔ Built-In USB-C Battery & SOS Light`
2. **Short Section Headers Everywhere (`3–5` Words Max — Never a Full Sentence):**
   - **Bad (13-word sentence):** `"Why 2,080+ Families & Campers Upgraded From Gravity Bags & Suction Straws"`
   - **Good (3–5 words):** `"See It In Action"` · `"Why This Is For You"` · `"Us vs. Old-School Filters"` · `"What Our Customers Say"` · `"30-Day Risk-Free Trial"` · `"Frequently Asked Questions"`
3. **Top-to-Bottom Persuasion Arc (Features at Top -> `"Why This Is For You"` Below):**
   - **Top (Hero Gallery + Hero Checkmarks):** Explains **what the product does and its core features**.
   - **Below the Videos (`features` block -> `"Why This Is For You"`):** Never explain the technical features a second time! Instead, show **3 real-life buyer situations** explaining why this product is made for their home, family, and emergencies (e.g., *When Storms & Boil-Water Alerts Empty Store Shelves*, *When Your Whole Family Needs Water — Not a Personal Straw*, *When You're On the Highway or Miles Off the Grid*) paired with **clean, text-free situational photos (`why-1.webp`–`why-3.webp`)**.
