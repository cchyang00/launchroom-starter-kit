## 2. Non-Negotiable Copywriting Rules

1. **Step 0 — Deconstruct Winning Viral Videos, Competitor Research & 4–5 Star Amazon Reviews First (Zero Scratch Invention):**
   - Read `research/<round>/buyers/L###.md`, `products/P###-slug/PRODUCT.md`, and the downloaded viral video transcripts/captions.
   - Extract:
     1. **Spoken Mechanism & Numbers from Viral Videos:** Exact phrases creators say to explain the mechanism (e.g., `"5-step filtration process that removes glass shards and minerals down to 0.01 micron"`), plus their 3-second opening hooks and visual demo moments.
     2. **Two-Layer Competitor Superiority:** How our exact mechanism beats established category alternatives (e.g., 1-touch electric pump vs. kneeling in mud with a plastic straw or waiting hours for a gravity bag) and why fake under-priced scam stores fail.
     3. **Top 3 Buyer-Praised Features & Top 4–6 Objections:** Pulled verbatim from 4–5 star and 2–4 star Amazon reviews.
   - Build the **`sbc-statics-2.0` 4-Column Concept Ledger** (`Feature -> What It Actually Does -> Practical Benefit -> Why It Matters in Their Day / Crisis`) in `products/P###-slug/creative/COPY-BRIEF.md`.
2. **HARD RULE — The 20-Static Ad Formula (`10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware`) + `sbc-statics-2.0` Copy Pack (`NATIVE-AD-KIT.md`):**
   - Never write random static ad concepts. Architect every product's static ad brief in `products/P###-slug/creative/NATIVE-AD-KIT.md` as **20 structured `4:5` static ads**:
     - **Ads `#1–#10` (`10 Problem-Aware / Unaware Ads`):** Make cold scrollers aware of a hidden problem or domino-chain crisis they never knew existed (*"First the bottled water at the store is gone. Then the food shelves empty. Next the power cuts out... When night falls on your street, what will you do?"*), mapped to `winning-statics` families `F8` (Problem-Agitation), `F2` (Illustrated Mechanism Diagrams), `F7` (Editorial/Insider Notes), and `F1` (Photographic Before/After).
     - **Ads `#11–#20` (`10 Solution-Aware / Competitor-Crushing Ads`):** Built from the viral video's mechanism proof + competitor research + 4–5 star reviews to prove why our product is way better than theirs, mapped to `F4` (Us-vs-Them Comparison), `F3` (Annotated Product Callouts), `F5` (Testimonials/Post-Its/AMA), and `F6`/`F9` (Big-Claim Hero & Checklists).
   - Pair the 20 statics with the **`sbc-statics-2.0` Copy Pack**:
     - **5 Distinct Evergreen Long-Form Primary Text Captions (`250–400 words` each)** written in 1st-person Avatar Persona POV (`Olivia Scott` or `Michael L`): (1) Unaware Domino-Chain Wake-Up, (2) 1st-Person Buyer Discovery & Relief Story, (3) Competitor Teardown & Mechanism Deep-Dive, (4) Skeptical Buyer Objection Crusher, and (5) 2-Kit Family/Vehicle Readiness Stack.
     - **5 Short High-CTR Meta Ad Headlines (`4–9 words` core hook + urgent reveal CTA)**.
3. **HARD RULE — Website vs. Meta Ad Headline Split (Owner Rule, Nov 3–5):**
   - **In Meta Ads (`#/create` & `os/data/meta-campaigns.json`):** The video or static already shows the product, so `Headline` MUST be a **short `4–9` word urgent 1st-person reveal/outcome hook + `"— Get Yours Now Today!"` CTA** (e.g., `"You Have to Get This 2027 Journal — Get Yours Now Today! 🌙"`, `"Keep Your Family Supplied With Clean Water in Any Outage ⚡"`).
   - **On the Website (`merrivy.com`, `hero.headline` & `purchase.heading`):** **NEVER** repeat *"You have to get this..."* on `hero.headline` or `purchase.heading` (the ad already did that job). Use **elevated, prestigious boutique or specialized hardware titles** (`"The Witching Hour 2027 Grimoire Planner"`, `"The Split-Panel Solar Indoor & Outdoor Blackout Ceiling Kit"`) while weaving viral creator reveal language into `purchase.hook` and the 3 `demonstrations.items[]` video titles.
4. **HARD RULE — Avatar Persona Pages & 1st-Person Buyer Voice (`Primary Text` & `Description`):**
   - Assign **`Olivia Scott`** (`page_id: 506843939180283`) for female-skewed / lifestyle / comfort products, or **`Michael L`** (`page_id: 1092000083990611`) for male / homeowner / preparedness / hardware products.
   - Never write dry specification bullet lists in ad copy.
5. **HARD RULE — 5-Section Grimoire Blueprint & Strictly 2-Kit Offer Copy (`1-Kit` vs `2-Kit` + `$4.99` Shipping Protection):**
   - Populate `hydrogen-storefront/app/content/<slug>.json` with `"sections": ["features", "comparison", "quotes", "guarantee", "faq"]`, 3 vertical creator demo videos (`demonstrations`), and 6 `.is-wftn-reviews` verified customer review cards (`quotes`).
   - Write `purchase.offerChoices` strictly for **`1-Kit`** (clean base retail price on the hero card; never show `+$7.99 Shipping` on the hero selector) and **`2-Kit`** (`★ MOST POPULAR · BEST VALUE`, `Save 15% + FREE Tracked Shipping`), plus the **`$4.99` Shipping Protection** toggle in the Cart Drawer (`addOnIds: []`). Never write 3-Kit or multi-SKU companion add-on copy.

## 4. Never

- Invent static ad angles from scratch without deconstructing the winning viral videos' spoken mechanism and competitor research into `10 Problem-Aware` + `10 Solution-Aware` statics.
- Put *"You have to get this..."* in `hero.headline` or `purchase.heading` on `merrivy.com`.
- Write vague opening hooks (*"With everything happening in the government..."*), lazy 1-sentence ad captions, or 14–20 word static ad headlines.
- Put dry feature/spec lists in Meta Ad `Headline`, `Primary Text`, or `Description`.
- Write 3-Pack / 3-Kit offers, multi-SKU companion add-on bundles, or `+$7.99 Shipping` on the front-page hero selector.
- Violate Meta Personal Attributes policy (*"Do you have [condition]?"*, medical cure/treatment claims) or invent fake reviews/ratings.
