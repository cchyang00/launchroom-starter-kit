## Review and learn

- Can the owner tell who this is for without an explanation?
- Does the image accurately show the product and proposed use?
- Can every factual promise be traced to the table above?
- Are objections answered with supported facts?
- Which angle did we choose, and why?
- What changed after the eye test?
- What remains a hypothesis, and what would disprove it?

Record reviewer, date, actual feedback and resulting revision. Agent self-review is not owner acceptance. Review the overall impression for unsupported results, contents, endorsements and urgency; a disclaimer does not automatically repair the main message.
