## 8. Words bank: every quote in one of four buckets

The concept cards at Step 4 are written from this bank. Every quote keeps its link and date (the number from section 9).

| Bucket | What goes here | Quotes (# from section 9) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger moment | When it hits ("awake at 3 a.m. counting the hours"), or the event that moved them from not noticing to shopping | |
| What they want | The better day they describe | |

| Their words | Phrases to echo in copy, never to fake as a review | |

**Intent tags** (optional, one per row in section 9): `need` (the problem said plainly) · `fit` ("will it work for me?") · `price` (pushback or anchor) · `compare` ("X or Y?") · `request` ("I wish it had...") · `proof` ("does it really work?") · `where` ("link?"). Many `fit`, `compare` and `price` tags point to a considered purchase (section 8b).

**Never:** diagnosis or condition words aimed at the reader, cure or treatment claims, before-and-after promises.
